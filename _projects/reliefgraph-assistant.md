## Introduction

This project aims to create an **AI-powered humanitarian intelligence platform** that streamlines how organizations collect, analyze, and interpret crisis-related data. Currently, humanitarian agencies often rely on **manual data gathering and fragmented reporting**, which leads to delays, inconsistencies, and limited situational awareness.  

Our system addresses these issues by **automating data extraction, integration, and analysis** from diverse sources such as news reports, social media, and open datasets. Through **natural language processing (NLP)** and **knowledge graph technologies**, the platform identifies key entities (e.g., locations, organizations, disaster types) and their relationships, transforming unstructured data into **structured, actionable insights**.  

By offering **real-time visual analytics and AI-driven summaries**, the project enables decision-makers to **detect emerging crises faster**, **coordinate cross-agency responses**, and **allocate resources more efficiently**. Moreover, built-in transparency features ensure that every piece of information is **traceable to its source**, promoting **accountability and trust** among global partners.  

Ultimately, the project enhances **situational awareness**, **reduces manual workloads**, and supports **evidence-based humanitarian operations**, contributing to more **effective and timely crisis response worldwide**.
